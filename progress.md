# 2026-08-24 P3-B1 source-local declaration decision

- P3-B1 must not use `graph_entities` as the parent of an exact entity mention.
  During P3-A materialization, global identity linking deliberately skips an
  unhinted same-name entity when a hinted identity exists, and it does not
  preserve every parser-local declaration-to-global-ID mapping.  Reusing that
  map would silently turn a source-exact mention index into a sparse global
  identity graph.
- Schema-v4 will therefore add a source-local, one-label-per-source declaration
  parent.  Its identifier is deterministically derived from user/source/label,
  rather than from a model- or global-identity ID, and it has a compound
  raw-source FK.  Child mention witnesses will use compound FKs to that
  declaration and raw source, retain canonical plus safely remapped raw spans,
  and fail closed on normalization-boundary ambiguity.  P3-B1 retrieval will
  resolve frozen query labels directly to source-local declarations/mentions
  and `DISTINCT` raw rows; it will not invoke P3-A global seed resolution or
  relation traversal.
- This is a no-model architectural decision only.  No database materialization,
  Search evaluation, commit, push, or merge has occurred.
